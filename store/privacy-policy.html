Picky Saver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

Your Privacy

Picky Saver does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. All photos remain on your device at all times.

Photo Access

Picky Saver requests access to your photo library solely to display, organize, and delete photos at your direction. Photos are never uploaded, shared, or sent to any server.

No Analytics or Tracking

Picky Saver does not include any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The app does not communicate with any external servers.

Data Storage

The only data stored locally on your device is your app preferences (such as whether you have seen the onboarding screen). This data is stored using your device's standard storage and is never transmitted.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at the support email listed on the app store page.
